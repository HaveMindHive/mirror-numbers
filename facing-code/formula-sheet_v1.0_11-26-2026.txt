UNITS AND MIRRORS
 1. Units:            u(1),u(2),u(3) = 1,2,3;  u(2k) = 2u(k);  u(2k+1) = 2u(k) + u(k+1)                       [L]
 2. One picture:      a,b,a : s  =  b + (s-1)a   (s=1 centre alone, s=2 halving, s=3 thirding; halving hinge b=a) [C n<5000]
 3. Imbalance:        u(n) = n + (sum of the wings of every filled-centre triple)                               [L]
 4. Self-count:       u(n) = n  exactly at 2^a and 3*2^a                                                       [L]
 5. Ratio-two line:   u(n) != 2n;  odd n below 2n exactly: 2^a+1, 3*2^a+1, and 1, 11, 15                         [L]
 6. Strands:          u(2^a+1) = 2^(a+1) - 1;   u(3*2^a+1) = 2(3*2^a - 1)                                        [L]
 7. Mod-4 law:        odd n: u odd iff n = 1 or 2^a+1;  u = 2 mod 4 iff n = 3*2^a+1;  else 4 | u(n)               [L]
 8. Step:             d(n) = 2u(n) - u(n+1):  d(2k) = d(k),  d(2k+1) = 4u(k)   (k = 0 or k >= 2)                [L]
 9. Class by 8:       u(8k+1) = 14u(k)+u(k+1); u(8k+3) = 16u(k)+4u(k+1); u(8k+5) = 12u(k)+8u(k+1); u(8k+7) = 8u(k)+12u(k+1)  [C k<20000]
10. Octave sum:       sum of u over [2^a, 2^(a+1)) = (17*5^a - 5*2^a)/15                                       [C a<18]
11. Pure halving:     with 3 = 1,2,1: v(n) = n iff 2^a; v != 2n; odd below 2n iff 1, 7, 2^a+1                    [L]
12. Shared wings:     halving wing = thirding wing  iff  n = 2, 3, 5                                           [L]

PRIMES THROUGH THE MIRRORS
13. Every prime a mirror: n has an equal split (q copies of n/q)  iff  n is not prime                           [C n<20000]
14. First mirror of a prime's centre (p+1)/2:  share f(q) = P(q)/(q-1),  P(q) = prod over primes r<q of (r-2)/(r-1), r=2 term 1/2
                      f(2)=1/2, f(3)=1/4, f(5)=1/16, f(7)=1/32, f(11)=1/64, f(13)=3/256, f(17)=33/4096        [C to 1e7, within ~1%]
15. Split hand-off:   consecutive primes p<q, centre m=(p+q)/2, half gap h:  exactly one of m, h is even;
                      exactly one is a multiple of 3;  for r >= 5 never both                                    [C to 2e6]
16. Centre tell:      mirror r through the centre forbids h = 0 mod r; a mirror off the centre forbids h = +-m mod r.
                      First allowed h = real half gap: 46.5% (mirrors 2,3), 73.2% (to 7), 89.3% (to 23)          [C to 2e6]
17. Gap counts:       N(g) ~ A * M(g) * exp(-g/L),  M(g) = prod over odd primes r | g of (r-1)/(r-2)
                      (gap 6 weight 2, gap 10 4/3, gap 30 8/3); L tracks the mean gap (10.97 to 2e6, 13.44 to 2e7);
                      gaps 4..60 within 18% at 2e7, tightening with size                                         [F]
